In the edit, the cultivated 16mm material was tested across several modes of overlaying — superimposition, double exposure, blending with digital footage, and frame-by-frame compositing. Each mode produced a different relationship between the biological film and the recorded fiction, and a different claim about what the mold was doing to the image. This folder documents the editing experiments, timeline screenshots, and the decisions that led to the final treatment of the 16mm material within the assembled film.
